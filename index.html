Real Estate Dashboard

A private Mac and iPhone app for a small real estate team.

Real Estate Dashboard helps our team keep track of listings, clients and showings. It brings together the email and calendar accounts the team already uses so nothing falls through the cracks:

  • Reads incoming email (read-only) to spot showing requests, client messages and attachments.
  • Adds showing holds to the team's Google Calendar and checks when the calendar is free.
  • Matches email with the team's contacts in Cloze.
  • Optionally files important email attachments into the team's own Dropbox.

The app is used only by members of our team. It is not sold or offered to the public.
