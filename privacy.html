Privacy Policy

Real Estate Dashboard · Last updated November 17, 2026

Real Estate Dashboard is a private app used only by members of our real estate team. This page explains what the app accesses, what it does with it, and where it is kept.

What the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only). When a team member connects a Gmail account, the app reads its messages and attachments. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar. When a team member connects a Google Calendar, the app lists the calendars that account can edit, checks when they are busy, searches for existing showings, and adds or updates showing events.
  • Google account email address. Used only to label the connected account in the app.
  • Other services the team connects, such as Microsoft Outlook, Cloze and Dropbox, are accessed in the same way, only with the team member's permission.

How the information is used

Email and calendar data is used only to provide the app's features to the team member who connected the account: finding showing requests and client messages, matching email with the team's contacts, placing and checking showing holds on the calendar, and, if turned on, saving attachments to the team's own Dropbox. Email is analyzed on the device. We do not use the data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on the team's own devices and in the team's private Apple iCloud storage so it can sync between those devices. Sign-in tokens and keys are kept in the device's Keychain and never leave that device. There is no separate server run by the app.

Sharing

We do not share Google user data with anyone outside the team, except with the services the team itself connects (for example, saving an attachment to the team's Dropbox) or when required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Real Estate Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A team member can disconnect an account at any time in the app (Inbox › Accounts › Disconnect), or remove the app's access from their Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data the app saved can be deleted by removing it in the app or deleting the app and its iCloud data.
